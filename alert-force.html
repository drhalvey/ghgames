How to play

The sortie: run to your B-52 and climb aboard, start the engines, take off, fill up from the tanker, check the go code, then fly low over enemy land to the target. Release the weapon over the yellow circle, turn away from the flash and fly to the exit point.

Stay low! Enemy radar cannot see you below about 70 m, or behind hills. The ENEMY RADAR bar fills when they can see you. When it fills, fighters come after you and missile sites can lock on.

Crew stations (1, 2, 3 or the buttons at the bottom). The crew do the other jobs for you, but not as well as you.

🧑‍✈️ Pilot: arrows or WASD to steer and climb, Shift for more throttle and Z for less, B to release the weapon.

📡 EW officer: watch the radar scope. C chaff beats radar missiles from sites, X flares beat heat missiles from fighters, J jammer hides you (it gets hot), V launches a decoy drone. Fire them when the missile is close, not too early. These keys work from any station.

🎯 Tail gunner: aim with the mouse or arrows, click or Space to fire at fighters behind you. Aim at the red dashed circle.

On phones and tablets use the stick on the left and the buttons on the right.
