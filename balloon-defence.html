You are the hero! Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or the joystick on an iPad. Your hero throws darts at the closest balloon all by itself (even metal and camo ones). Popping balloons gives your hero XP: at level 3 you unlock Spin (Q), at level 6 Freeze (E) and at level 10 Meteor (R). Pick a tower from the left side, then tap a grass square to build it. Tap a tower to upgrade it, change who it aims at, or sell it. Each tower has two upgrade paths, and only one can go all the way to tier 3. Metal balloons shrug off sharp darts, so use bombs, zaps or a powered-up Owl. Camo balloons can only be seen by the Owl Scout, a Frog with Frog Eyes or a Zapbot with Crystal Eye. A boss blimp arrives every 10 rounds! Your run saves between rounds. Every round you survive pays coins (better maps pay more). Coins buy hero upgrades, new maps (later maps need rebirths), hero outfits with multis and pet eggs. Pets follow your hero and pop balloons too. Rebirth resets coins and hero upgrades but keeps maps, outfits and pets, and adds +1x hero damage and coins for ever. Your highest round goes on the leaderboard.
