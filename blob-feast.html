Mouse: point where to wobble. Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D. iPad: drag anywhere to steer with the joystick. You can soak up anything clearly smaller than you; bigger things block your way. The bar shows how close you are to the next size of thing. Computer blobs roam the same town: soak up the smaller ones, but keep away from bigger ones or they squish some of your jelly away. Each round lasts two minutes (more with upgrades), then you get coins for everything you soaked up and how big you got. Coins buy upgrades (speed, round time, start size, magnet pull, growth, coins), skins with multis and pet eggs. Pets orbit your blob and pull things in. Reach a big enough size to unlock the next world: toy town, farm, city, beach, theme park and space station. Rebirth resets your coins and upgrades for a bigger multi for ever. The leaderboard is the biggest size ever.
