Mouse: point where to wobble. Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D. iPad: drag anywhere to steer with the joystick. The town is giant, so the corner map shows you (in the middle), the town edge, computer blobs (green ones you can soak up, red ones can squish you) and the gold Mega Blobs; the little square shows where you are in the whole town. You can soak up anything clearly smaller than you; bigger things block your way, and the bigger you get the faster you wobble. You are safe for the first few seconds, then bigger blobs hunt you and try to cut you off. If one touches you it squishes away a big chunk of your jelly, and if you get squished smaller than you started the round ends. Soak up a crowned Mega Blob for a huge bonus, and grow big enough to soak up the biggest things in the world to become a Town Crusher. Each round lasts 90 seconds (more with upgrades), then you get coins for everything you soaked up and how big you got. Coins buy upgrades (speed, round time, start size, magnet pull, growth, coins), skins with multis and pet eggs. Pets orbit your blob and pull things in. Reach a big enough size to unlock the next world: toy town, farm, city, beach, theme park and space station. Rebirth resets your coins and upgrades for a bigger multi for ever. The leaderboard is the biggest size ever.
