BLOB FEAST

Your jelly blob follows your mouse or finger. Gobble the dots and any blob a bit smaller than you. Run from bigger ones! Space splits you in two to pounce, W shoots out a little mass. Big blobs that touch a spiky green virus burst into bits.

Gobbled!

Mouse or finger: your blob heads towards it. Arrow keys also steer. Space or SPLIT: split in two and fling half forward (your pieces join back together after a while). W or SHOOT: shoot out a bit of mass, handy for feeding viruses so they shoot at others. You must be about 10% bigger than a blob to eat it. Bigger blobs are slower. Your best mass goes on the leaderboard, and big milestones or topping the arena earn PhatCoin.
