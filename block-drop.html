BLOCK DROP

Real 3D shapes fall into a deep pit. Move them, turn them every way and fill a whole layer to blast it away for coins!

Paused

Pit full!

Keys: arrows or WASD move the shape (left, right, away, near, matching the view). Q and E spin it, Z tips it forwards, X rolls it sideways, hold Shift to fall faster and press Space to drop. C turns the camera 90 degrees and L fires the Line Laser. On iPad use the buttons, or swipe on the pit to move and tap to spin. Fill every square of a layer to clear it. Coins buy upgrades: Slow Fall, more Preview pieces, Bomb pieces (they blast a 3x3x3 area), the Ghost piece that shows where you will land, and the Line Laser that zaps your top layer. Pets sit on the edge of the pit and sometimes drop you a handy single block or a bomb. Worlds have bigger pits and bigger coin multis, and rebirth gives a permanent coin multi. The leaderboard is most layers cleared in one game.
