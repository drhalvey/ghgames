GAME OVER

Roll the dice and go round the board. Land on a street, train line or power and water that nobody owns and you can buy it, or it goes to auction. Land on someone else's place and you pay them rent. Own every street of one colour to double the rent and build cottages (then a tower) to make it much bigger. Pass Payday to collect $200. Headline and Lucky Dip cards can help or hurt. Three doubles in a row, or landing on Busted!, sends you to the Lock-up: pay $50, use a card, or try to roll doubles. Trade with other players, mortgage places when you need cash, and the last one not bankrupt wins (or the richest when the rounds run out). In Economics mode you can also borrow from the bank, save for interest, watch the Reserve Bank change interest rates, ride property booms and busts, and buy gold. Tap any square to see its deed. 🗺️ flips between the 3D table and a flat board. Your game saves by itself.

About Boomtown

Boomtown is a 3D property trading board game set in Perth. Buy suburbs from Armadale to Peppermint Grove, build cottages and towers, charge rent and trade your way to a fortune against the computer or friends online.

How to play

  1. Roll the dice and move round the board, collecting $200 every time you pass Payday.
  2. Buy streets, train lines and utilities nobody owns, or bid for them at auction.
  3. Own a whole colour set to double the rent, then build cottages and towers for much more.
  4. Trade with other players, mortgage places when cash is short, and stay out of the Lock-up.
  5. Be the last one standing, or the richest when the rounds run out.

Economics mode

Three levels teach how money works: borrowing and saving with interest and a credit rating; the Reserve Bank, credit supply and property booms and busts; and gold, including the old gold standard where banknotes were gold certificates.
