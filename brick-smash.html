BALLS

WELCOME BACK!

⚽💥🧱

Your balls bounce around the board all by themselves, smashing bricks. Every brick shows its HP, and breaking it pays coins. Tap or click bricks to hit them yourself. Buy new ball types in the Balls shop: Basic, Fast, Splash (hits the bricks around it), Sniper (flies straight at the toughest brick), Poison (keeps hurting bricks after it hits) and Cannon (smashes straight through). Each ball type has its own Damage, Speed and Count upgrades. Every 10th level is a boss brick! Clearing a level pays a bonus, and the next level's bricks are tougher. Upgrades help your taps, crits, coins and ball speed. Worlds give much bigger coins (later ones need rebirths), pets multiply all ball damage, and skins add small multis. Rebirth takes you back to level 1 and resets coins, balls and upgrades, but keeps worlds, skins and pets and gives a giant damage multi. While you are away your balls keep earning (up to 8 hours). The leaderboard is the highest level you reach.
