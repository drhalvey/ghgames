How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD. The camera follows behind you: drag with the mouse to look around, and the mouse wheel zooms. On a tablet, drag the left side of the game to move and drag the right side to look.
  • Attack: Space or the big round button. Dash: E key or the yellow DASH button.
  • Pedigreers 🐶 jump up and SLAM the ground. Every bro close by gets hit. Big damage, but it takes a moment to charge again.
  • Bro Got Slammed 😎 swing the bat 🏏 in the direction you are moving. Smaller hits, but really quick.
  • Pass out: Pedigreers have 150 health and bros have 250. When yours runs out you pass out 💫 and stay down until the next round.
  • Win: each round lasts 4 minutes. The team with more players still standing at the end wins. Knock out the whole other team and you win straight away. Same number left is a draw.
  • Wins 🏆: everyone on the winning team gets a win, even if they passed out. Spend wins on damage upgrades and Win multi below. Win multi gives you more wins every time your team wins.
  • Same wins and upgrades as the Classic version, and 3D and Classic players fight in the same games.

Upgrades

Spend your wins to hit harder. Bat power works when you are a bro, Slam power when you are a Pedigreer. Friends online feel it too. Win multi gives you extra wins every time your team wins.
