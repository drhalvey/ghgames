Friends who pick the same map play in the same game. Computer players fill any empty spots.

Pick a team and a map, then press PLAY. Each round lasts 5 minutes.

How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD. On a tablet, touch and drag anywhere on the game.
  • Dash: Space or the pink DASH button. Then wait for it to charge again.
  • Dads start frozen for 3 seconds while the kids run. Then they chase. A hug is so tight the kid turns into a dad.
  • Soda: there are 6 soda machines 🥤 on every map and the soda is free. Kids walk into a machine to grab one, then touch a dad. He drinks it and passes out 💤. Dads, keep away from kids carrying soda!
  • Boss: when every dad is down, Monica arrives riding Didi, her massive greyhound 🐕. If Didi catches you, you get slobbered and you are out.
  • Beat the boss: grab a peanut butter jar 🥜 (Didi will chase you!) and press DROP (E key) to lure her. While Didi slurps for 10 seconds, every kid gets TNT 🧨. Place it around her (E key) and it blows up 2 seconds later. Enough blasts and the kids win.
  • Win: kids win if someone is still free when the 5 minutes run out, or if they beat Monica and Didi. Dads win if every kid gets hugged. Monica wins if Didi slobbers every kid.
  • Points: kids get 1 point a second while free, 40 for each dad knocked out with soda, 50 for luring Didi, 30 for each TNT blast that hits her, 200 for escaping and 300 for beating the boss. Dads get 50 for every hug. Every 5 points gives you a sock 🧦.
  • Maps: water and sand slow you down, slides, trampolines and hot sand speed you up, and ice makes you slide.

Hat shop

Spend socks on hats. Your hat shows on your character, and friends online can see it.

Gifts

Type a friend's in-game name and send them socks or one of your hats. You can only give what you have, and it leaves your game straight away. Gifts arrive while their Crazy Dads game is open. Both of you need a name set.
