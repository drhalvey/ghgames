Your cubes, coins and upgrades go and you go back to 30 coins. You keep your rebirths, worlds, pets and skins.

Tap Buy to buy a cube. A 1 cube earns 1 coin a second, a 2 cube earns 2, and so on. Drag a cube onto one with the same number to merge it, tap one then the other, or press Merge!. Each new cube you make changes the shop to sell that cube, at 2.5 times the price. Keys: B or Space buys, M or Enter merges. The conveyor at the back drops free cubes now and then, and a golden cube sometimes floats by: tap it for a Gold Rush (triple coins). Upgrades: bigger floor, faster conveyor, better conveyor cubes, coin boost and golden luck. Pets sit on your floor: some merge cubes for you, all of them boost your coins. Rebirth resets coins, cubes and upgrades for a permanent coin multi, and worlds give even bigger multis. Your cubes keep earning while you are away (up to 8 hours). The leaderboard is most rebirths.
