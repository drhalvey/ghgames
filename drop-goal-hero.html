How to play

  • The scrum-half passes you the ball. Straight away the defenders start charging at you.
  • Aim with the arrow buttons, the arrow keys, or by dragging on the pitch.
  • Tap DROP (or space) to drop the ball. Time slows down.
  • Tap KICK when the ball touches the grass, right as the shrinking ring meets the ball. That's a PERFECT kick.
  • Kick too early and it's a punt, not a drop goal. Too late and it's a scuff, or you get charged down.
  • Watch the wind flag. It pushes the ball left or right.
  • 2003 Final: 26 seconds left, 17-17. One kick to win the World Cup.
  • Career: every goal moves you further away. Coins buy a stronger leg and new stadiums.
