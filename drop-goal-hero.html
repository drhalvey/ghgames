How to play

  • Drop goals: watch the forwards carry it up. When the scrum-half passes to you, the defence rushes.
  • Aim with the arrows (or drag on the pitch). Tap DROP, then tap KICK just as the ball hits the grass, when the ring meets the ball.
  • Too early is a punt, which doesn't count. Too late is a scuff, or you get charged down.
  • Conversions: after the try, slide the ball further back or closer on the line it was scored on. Then aim.
  • Tap START KICK. Stop the POWER bar near the top, then stop the needle in the gold zone. Your kicker runs in and strikes it.
  • Watch the wind. The touch judges behind the posts put their flags up when it's over.
  • 2003 Final: 17-17 in extra time with 26 seconds left. One drop goal wins the World Cup.
  • Career: drop goals and conversions take turns, each one harder. Coins buy upgrades and stadiums.
  • 3D camera: drag the pitch to swing the TV camera round (Q and E on a keyboard), mouse wheel or pinch to zoom. While aiming, sideways drags aim. A and D aim too.
  • Same save as the Classic (2D) version.
