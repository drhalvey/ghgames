How to play

Knock out the other team before they knock you out. Each round, everyone starts with full health. The first side to win 5 rounds wins the match. Headshots do extra damage.

Computer: click the game to grab the mouse. W A S D to move, mouse to look, click to shoot, right-click to aim (snipers zoom in), Space to jump, R to reload, 1 2 3 4 or the mouse wheel to change weapon, Q to throw your utility straight away, Tab for the scores, Esc for the menu.

iPad and phone: drag the left side to walk, drag the right side to look, and hold FIRE to shoot (you can drag the FIRE button to aim while you shoot). Tap the weapon buttons at the bottom to swap.

You get 🔑 keys for knockouts, round wins and match wins. Keys buy new weapons and finishing moves, and open cases for wraps. Ranked matches move you from Bronze up to Legend. The Public Arena is everyone online at once, free for all.

George's duelling game: 1v1 duels, team battles from 2v2 to 5v5, ranked from Bronze to Legend, a Public Arena with everyone online and room codes to play with friends. 30 weapons, 25 wraps, 14 finishing moves and 12 maps. Your progress saves, and with a GH Games account it saves online too.
