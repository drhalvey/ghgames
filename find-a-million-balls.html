FIND A MILLION BALLS

Balls keep popping up in hiding spots all over five worlds: up trees, on rooftops, inside houses, under benches and in secret places. Grab them for cash, upgrade your luck to find rarer ones, and collect all 500 kinds in your Ball Book. Earn gems, build pushers, find mutations, and rebirth for x3 luck every time!

BALL BOOK COMPLETE!

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys, jump with Space. Drag the screen to look around (Q and E turn too, the mouse wheel zooms). On iPad: drag the left side to move, drag the right side to look, tap JUMP, and tap a ball to grab it. Balls keep spawning in the world you are in. Every world has 100 kinds of ball, from Common (1 in 2) to Secret (1 in billions). Luck makes rare balls spawn more, Cash Multi gives more cash, Max Balls lets more spawn at once and Spawn Speed makes them pop up faster. Rare balls shine a light beam so you can find them. You also earn 25 💎 gems every second (Gem Multi goes up to x10): spend them in the 💎 Gem Shop on Luck and Cash Multis and on auras. Buy pushers (🏭) with cash: they push balls off the edge for cash, each one bigger than the last. Balls can spawn with one of 12 mutations, from Shiny (x1.5 cash) to Celestial (x1000). Rebirth for a x3 luck multi each time (x3, x9, x27, x81...). New kinds go in the 📖 Ball Book and open the gates to new worlds. Your game saves by itself.
