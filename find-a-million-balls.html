FIND A MILLION BALLS

Balls rain down onto a floating island. Grab your pusher and shove them off the edge into the Void! Every ball you push in gives you cash. Buy bigger pushers and upgrades, roll abilities with gems, find all 500 kinds of ball in Normal and in every one of the 12 mutations for your Index (6,500 in all), rebirth for x3 luck, and push ONE MILLION balls.

ONE MILLION!

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys, jump with Space, use your ability with E. Drag the screen to look around (the mouse wheel zooms). On iPad: drag the left side to move, drag the right side to look, tap JUMP and ⚡. Your pusher is the board in front of you: walk into balls to shove them off the island into the Void. Every ball pushed in counts towards one million and pays cash; rarer balls pay much more (Common x1 up to Secret x1000) and mutations multiply it again (Shiny x1.5 up to Celestial x1000). Shop: cash upgrades (Cash Multi, Walk Speed, Faster Drops, Drop Count, Item Capacity, Luck, Gem Multi), 10 pushers each wider than the last, trails, and Rebirth (x3 luck each time). You earn 25 💎 gems a second and gems drop too: spend them on Luck and Cash Multis, auras and ability rolls (Wave, Gust, Conveyor, Prospector, Frenzy, Golden Touch). Your game saves by itself.
