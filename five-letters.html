FIVE LETTERS

Walk the path and battle friendly blob monsters by guessing their secret five-letter word. Green letters hit hard, yellow ones hit softer, and solving the word fires a finishing blast!

Knocked out!

Type on your keyboard or tap the letters, then press Enter. Words must be real five-letter words. After each guess the tiles turn green (right letter, right spot), yellow (in the word, wrong spot) or grey. Every guess hits the monster: green letters hit hard, yellow ones less, and your pets add their own hit. Between guesses the monster bops you back. Solve the word for a big finishing blast; if the monster is still standing it gets a new word. Run out of guesses and it gives you a big bop. Every 10th room is a boss. Coins buy Sharper Letters (more damage), an Extra Guess, Hint scrolls, a Bubble Shield, Heal Potions, more health and more coins. Beat a world's boss to open the next world (later ones need rebirths). The Daily word is the same for everyone and changes at midnight Perth time. The leaderboard is the furthest room reached.
