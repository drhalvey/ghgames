FLAP DASH

You're a sky courier! Fly through the green hoops to pick up parcels, then fly through the gold hoops on floating islands to deliver them, munching fruit to keep your stamina up.

Out of puff!

Tap the game, click, or press Space, Up or W to flap. Keep holding to glide gently, and hold Down or S (or the DIVE button on a tablet) to dive fast. Fly through a green 📦 hoop to fill your bag with parcels, then through the gold 🏠 hoop above an island house to deliver them. Each parcel pays coins. Flapping uses stamina: eat fruit to refill it, or land on an island top to rest. Lightning clouds zap you when they flash, windmill blades, grumpy nest birds and balloon sides bump you (bounce on top of a balloon for a free boing!). Bumps cost stamina and drop a parcel. When your stamina runs out the flight ends. Coins buy upgrades (stamina, glide, speed, bag size, tips and fruit power), new sky worlds, pet eggs and flyer skins. Pets fly with you and catch coins. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades but multiplies your coins for ever. The leaderboard is most deliveries in one flight.
