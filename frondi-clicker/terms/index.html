Frondi Clicker terms of use

Last updated 18 September 2026

Frondi Clicker is free to play, on iPhone and in a web browser at ghgames.au. By playing it, and especially by posting to the leaderboard, you agree to these terms.

Leaderboard rules

  • Keep your nickname friendly. No swearing, and nothing rude, mean, sexual, hateful or threatening.
  • Do not use anyone's real full name, address, school, phone number, email or any other personal detail as a nickname.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else.
  • Do not use the leaderboard to advertise anything.

There is no tolerance for names that break these rules. Names that break them are deleted, and a player who keeps breaking them can be blocked from posting scores.

Reporting

In the app, press and hold a name on the leaderboard to hide it on your own device or to report it. Reports go to ejhalvey@gmail.com and are acted on, usually within 24 hours. Anyone can also report a name by email.

Your game, your device

Your progress is saved on your own device. If you delete the app, clear your browser data or change device, that progress is gone and cannot be restored. The app and the website keep separate progress.

Fair play

The game keeps time with your device's clock, so changing the clock changes when events like Admin Abuse happen. Scores posted from an edited save or an altered clock may be removed from the leaderboard.

No warranty

The game is provided as it is, for fun. It may contain bugs, and the leaderboard may be unavailable at times. It is not sold, and nothing in it is bought or paid for.
