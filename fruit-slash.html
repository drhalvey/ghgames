FRUIT SLASH

Swipe to slice fruit and fill the juice tanks of your smoothie bar. Customers queue up and buy every smoothie for coins, so upgrade, hatch helpers and grow a giant smoothie empire!

Swipe with your finger, or hold the mouse button and swipe, to slice fruit. Every slice pours juice into the tanks on your counter, and customers buy smoothies from the tanks all by themselves, even while you shop. Slice 3 or more fruit in one swipe for a combo multi. Giant boss fruit float in now and then: slice them again and again to burst them for a huge splash of juice. Cartoon bombs spill some of your juice, so swipe around them. Upgrades: Bigger Blade, Combo Multi, More Customers, Juice Price, Bigger Tanks, Fruit Rain and the Auto-Slicer Robot. Coins also unlock 8 fruit farms with fancier fruit (later farms need rebirths), pet eggs (pets fly out and slice fruit for you) and new blades with multis. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades but keeps farms, blades and pets and gives a bigger multi for ever. Come back every day for a daily reward. The leaderboard is most rebirths.
