GLOBE GRAB

Roll two 9-sided dice round a giant board of 80 squares. Buy real cities, grab whole countries, build up to Mega Towers and Sky Castles, and watch out for the Sneaky Thief. Every lap the prices grow into giant numbers!

Who's playing (up to 6)
Game length
Computer players
Computer speed

You win!

Everyone starts with 1,500 and takes turns rolling two 9-sided dice (2 to 18). Land on a city nobody owns and you can buy it. Land on someone else's and you pay them rent. Own every city in a country (all 4) and rent doubles, and you can build: 4 houses, then a Hotel, then a Mega Tower, then a Sky Castle floating on a cloud. Airports charge more the more of them you own, and the Solar and Wind Farms charge by your dice roll. Land on a Sneaky Thief square to steal one property from another player (Mega Towers and Sky Castles are too big to steal). Postcards and Lucky Suitcases are surprise cards. Rolling a double gives you another go, but three doubles in a row, or landing on Go to Passport Control, sends you to Passport Control: roll a double, pay the fine or use a Fast Pass to get out. Every time someone finishes a lap ahead of everyone else, the whole world booms and every price, rent and pay jumps up: x10, then x1,000, then x1M, then x10B and on into giant numbers. Tap any square to see its rents, drag to look around, and press 🎥 for the whole board. If you run out of money your buildings and places are sold off to pay; if that's still not enough you're out. Last one in, or the richest when the rounds run out, wins. The leaderboard is most wins against computer players.
