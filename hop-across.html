HOP ACROSS

Tap to hop forward. Swipe, or use the arrow keys or W A S D, to hop any way. Dodge the cars, ride the logs, watch for the train lights, and do not dawdle: the world keeps moving!

CRITTERS

Spend the coins you pick up on new critters.

Squashed!

Tap the game (or press Space or Up) to hop forward. Swipe left, right or down to hop sideways or back. Cars and trucks squash you, water is a splash, and the flashing red lights mean a train is coming. Logs and lily pads carry you over rivers. Your score is how far you get. Pick up coins to unlock new critters. New best scores and big milestones earn PhatCoin.
