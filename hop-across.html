HOP ACROSS

Baby critters are stranded across busy roads, rivers and rails: hop to them and they follow you in a hopping conga line. Lead them to a safe nest to bank them, and the longer your conga, the more coins you get!

Squashed!

Tap the game (or press Space, Up or W) to hop forward. Swipe, or use the arrow keys or W A S D, to hop left, right or back. Baby critters sit waiting across the map: hop onto one and it joins your conga line. Every baby following you multiplies the coins you pick up. If a car or train bumps a follower it isn't hurt, it just runs back to where you found it. Hop onto a big nest row to bank your babies: the more you bring at once, the bigger the bonus. Cars, trains, deep water, lava and space void end the run unless you have a shield. Coins buy upgrades (hop speed, a longer conga, shields, a coin magnet and more), new worlds with their own hazards, pet eggs and critter skins. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades but multiplies your coins for ever. The leaderboard is most babies saved in one run.
