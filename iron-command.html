How to play

Build your base

  • Select your MCV truck and press Deploy to make a Construction Yard.
  • Tap a building on the right to start building it. When it says READY, tap it again, then tap the map to place it. Green is OK, red is not.
  • Build Power Plants first. If the power bar goes red, everything slows down and towers stop working.
  • A Refinery comes with a Harvester. Harvesters collect ore and turn it into money ($) by themselves.
  • Barracks make soldiers. A War Factory makes tanks. A Radar Dome turns on the map. A Tech Centre unlocks the best stuff.

Command your army

  • Tap a unit to select it. Tap the ground to move. Tap an enemy to attack it.
  • Drag with one finger to move around the map. Pinch to zoom.
  • Turn on Box then drag to select lots of units at once. Army selects all your fighters.
  • Attack mode: your units fight anything they meet on the way.
  • Select a Barracks or War Factory, then tap the ground to set where new units go.
  • Engineers take over enemy buildings, or fully fix your own.
  • Units get stripes after kills and become stronger.

On a computer

  • Click to select and order. Drag with the left button for a selection box. Right-drag or arrow keys/WASD to scroll. Mouse wheel zooms.
  • Keys: A attack-move, S stop, D deploy, Q select army, H go to base, Esc clear, Space pause.

Saving

Your battle saves by itself every 20 seconds and when you leave. Come back any time and press Continue battle. Log in (button at the top of the menu) to keep your saves on every device.
