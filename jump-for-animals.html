About Jump for Animals

Jump for Animals is a 3D jump simulator: every jump makes your jumps bigger, and every giant wall you clear wins you an animal that earns money.

How to play

  1. Jump to build up jump power.
  2. Run at a wall and jump over it once your jump power beats the number on the wall.
  3. Win the animal behind each of the 105 walls: 25 rarities, from a Common Mouse to a Supreme Star Kangaroo.
  4. Spend the money your animals make on items, from a pencil to a black hole, for bigger jump multis.
  5. Jump the purple Rebirth Wall (wall 15, then 10 more walls each rebirth) for x5 jump power and money, up to 10 rebirths and 105 walls.
