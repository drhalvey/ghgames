Victory!

Pick a boss on the battle map, buy army upgrades, then fight! Tap one of your pieces and tap a green dot to move (or drag it). Drag the empty table to spin the view. Checkmate the boss king to win, and if a king has no moves at all, that side loses. Every boss has special rules: ♻️ pieces that respawn, 🌫️ fog over their half, 👑 an extra queen, 🔟 a 10 by 10 board, 🏃 pawns that start further forward, 🛡️ shields and more. Shields block the first capture: the attacker bounces off and the shield breaks. Army upgrades: 💪 pawns that also step one square like a king, 🐴 knights that can jump twice, 🛡️ shields, 👑 a second queen and a coin bag. Capture pieces and beat bosses for coins. Pets give coin multis and a once-per-battle power (swap two of your pieces, freeze an enemy piece, add a shield, zap a pawn or undo). Rebirth resets coins and upgrades for a coin multi that lasts for ever, and new worlds need rebirths. The leaderboard is the furthest boss beaten. Classic chess (5 computer levels or 2 players) is still here: press Classic chess.
