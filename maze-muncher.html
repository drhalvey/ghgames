Swipe on the maze, use the arrow keys or W A S D, or the on-screen pad on a tablet. You can press a direction early and Chompo will turn at the next gap. The side tunnels take you to the other side. Power berries make the monsters blue and slow for a few seconds: eat them one after another for 200, 400, 800 and 1600 points. Bonus treats appear twice a level. Every level is a new maze and a bit faster. Coins you earn buy new looks for Chompo. Clearing levels earns PhatCoin.
