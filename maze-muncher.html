Swipe on the maze, use the arrow keys or W A S D, or the on-screen pad on a tablet. Press a direction early and you'll turn at the next gap. Rooms are hidden in fog until you walk in, and the map in the corner shows where you've been. Dots pay coins. The four monsters roam the dungeon and chase you when you're close: a big power berry turns them blue so you can munch them. Coloured doors need the key of the same colour, hidden somewhere in the rooms you can already reach. Treasure chests are full of coins. The crown door leads to the boss: grab the power berries that appear in the boss room, then munch the boss while it's scared, and dodge its goo blobs. Beating a boss unlocks the next world (later worlds also need rebirths). Coins buy upgrades (speed, power berry time, a dot magnet, extra lives, coin value and monster bounty), pet eggs and new looks. Pets follow you and eat nearby dots. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades but multiplies your coins for ever. The leaderboard is total bosses beaten.
