MEGA DROPPER
TYCOON

This plot is yours! Walk onto the green buy pads to build droppers, conveyors and upgraders. Droppers drop ore onto the conveyor, upgraders make it worth more, and the furnace turns it into cash. Step on the Collect pad to grab your cash, then build bigger. Hatch pets, unlock 8 worlds and rebirth for huge multis!

UPGRADES

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys, jump with Space, drag the screen to look around (the mouse wheel zooms, Q and E turn). On iPad: drag the left side to move and the right side to look. Walk onto a green buy pad to buy what it says (it turns red if you cannot afford it yet). Ore that reaches the furnace goes into your tycoon's bank: step on the gold 💰 Collect pad to collect it, or buy the Auto Collector. ⬆️ Upgrades: Ore Value, Drop Speed, Conveyor Speed and Walk Speed. 🔁 Rebirth: x3 cash every rebirth, but it resets your cash, your builds and upgrades (pets, worlds and Walk Speed are kept). 🥚 Pets multiply your cash. 🌍 8 worlds, each with ore worth way more. 👥 visits the tycoons next door. You earn half your cash per second while you are away (up to 2 hours). Your game saves by itself.
