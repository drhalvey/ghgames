MINE PATROL

Dig down for treasure! Numbers still tell you how many mines are hiding next to a block, but the dirt also hides coins, gems, keys and chests. Find the stairs to dig deeper, and mind your hearts!

Out of hearts!

Click or tap a block to dig it. Right-click, long-press, or switch to Flag mode to plant a flag where you think a mine is. Each number tells you how many mines touch that spot, and your first dig on every level is always safe. Hidden blocks can hold coins, gems, keys and chests: tap a chest when you have a key to open it. Hitting a mine costs one heart, not the whole game. Tap the stairs to go a level deeper, or dig every safe block for a Full Clear bonus. Tools: the Wide Shovel digs three in a row and flags any mines it finds, Safe Sense digs a safe block for you, and X-ray Goggles show the mines for a moment. Pets sometimes sniff out a mine and flag it. Every 10 levels is a new world (later ones need rebirths). Drag to turn the camera, pinch or scroll to zoom. The leaderboard is the deepest level reached.
