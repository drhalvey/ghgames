How to play

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys, or drag the left side of the screen on an iPad. Tap or click the ground to walk there. Turn the camera: drag with the mouse, or drag the right side of the screen. Mouse wheel or 🔍 zooms. The camera pulls back as you grow.
  • Attack: get close to another monster and you bite automatically. Tap a monster to chase it.
  • Eat zombies by running into them. Every zombie makes you bigger, tougher and gives coins. Big green Brutes, purple Giants and the huge Zombie Kings fight back, so bite them a few times first. Catch a shiny Golden zombie for loads of coins!
  • SMASH (Space) hits everything around you hard. DASH (Shift) lunges you forward to catch or escape.
  • Levels: be the last monster standing. After a while the purple storm closes in, so you have to fight. Every 10th level is a Boss. 10 worlds, 100 levels. More health left at the end means more stars.
  • Public Arena: everyone who is playing is in the same arena. Real players have a 🟢 by their name. Knock them out for big coins. If nobody is on, computer monsters fight you instead.
