This browser can't draw 3D (WebGL). Try Chrome or Safari, or another device.

Computer: click the battlefield to grab the mouse (Esc lets go). Mouse looks and aims (it slows a little when you are on a target). Zoom: mouse wheel, or right click / Z to step through. Machine gun: hold left click or Space to fire; zoom right in for the telescopic sight, R reload the belt, A calls the artillery. Rifleman: WASD move, Shift sprint, C duck (ducking inside a shell hole hides you from the machine guns), left click fire, right click (or wheel) to look down the iron sights, R reload, F bayonet, G throw a bomb.
iPad / phone: drag on the right side to aim, pinch with two fingers to zoom (or tap the zoom button), left thumb stick to move, buttons for everything else.
