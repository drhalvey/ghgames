Computer: click the battlefield to grab the mouse (Esc lets go). Mouse looks and aims (it slows a little when you are on a target). Zoom: mouse wheel, or right click / Z to step through. Machine gun: hold left click or Space to fire; zoom right in for the telescopic sight, R reload the belt, A fires an SOS flare for your artillery, C ducks below the parapet (do it when shells or bombs come), M gas mask, G throws a bomb bundle at a close tank. Planes can be shot down: aim ahead of them. Each battle tells its own day: the Somme for the German gunner, Operation Michael for the British. Rifleman: WASD move, Shift sprint, C duck (ducking inside a shell hole hides you from the machine guns), left click fire, right click (or wheel) to look down the iron sights, R reload, F bayonet, G throw a bomb. Once you hold their trench, E turns their machine gun round.
Machine gunner extras: Q swaps to your own rifle and back. While ducked (C), V puts up a trench periscope so you can watch without being shot (works for the rifleman too when ducked).
Night raider: creep across, freeze or duck when a flare goes up, keep out of the searchlight, grab the maps by the lantern and get home. The bayonet is silent; shots and bombs wake them up.
Tank commander: W / S drive, A / D steer, mouse aims, click fires the big gun, hold Space for the machine guns, right click for the gun sight.
Fighter pilot: mouse or A / D to turn, W / S to climb and dive, hold click or Space to fire, Shift to boost. Get behind them!
Kit shop: every battle earns battle pay. Buy a Lewis gun, tougher helmet, body armour, zoom periscope and more, with pay or PhatCoin.
iPad / phone: drag on the right side to aim, pinch with two fingers to zoom (or tap the zoom button), left thumb stick to move, buttons for everything else.

About Over the Top

Over the Top is a First World War game set on the Western Front from 1916 to 1918. Play as the British or the Germans.

How to play

  1. Pick a side and a role.
  2. On the machine gun, hold the trench against waves of attackers and keep the gun from overheating.
  3. As a rifleman, fix bayonets, go over the top and cross no man's land.
  4. On a night raid, sneak across past flares and a searchlight to grab their maps.
  5. Drive a tank over the wire, or fly a fighter to bust balloons and beat their ace.
  6. Earn career ranks and battle pay, and spend it in the Kit shop.
