Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, drag the mouse (or hold Q or Z) to turn the camera, click or F to PARRY, R or E for your ability, Space to jump. You aim where the camera looks: the yellow arrow shows who your parry will hit. iPad: drag on the left side to move, drag on the right side to turn, tap PARRY. The ball turns red when it's coming for you. Press PARRY just before it reaches you; if you press too early you have to wait a moment before you can try again. Every parry makes the ball faster. If the ball you hit knocks someone out, you get the knockout and your explosion plays. 1v1 Son vs Dad on one device: Son uses WASD and F, Dad uses the arrows and L (or Enter); on iPad tap your own PARRY button. First to 3 wins. Coins buy upgrades, sword and explosion crates (swords multiply all coins, explosions multiply knockout coins), abilities and maps. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades but doubles your coins for ever. The leaderboard is most rounds won.
