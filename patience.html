Tap a level on the world map, then clear every card from the table. You can tap any face-up card that is not covered, if it is one higher or one lower than your pile card (King and Ace join up). Tap the deck to draw a new pile card when you are stuck, but drawing ends your streak. Long streaks multiply your coins. Special cards: 💣 bombs blow away the cards next to them, 🌈 wild cards go on anything and anything goes on them, ⭐ golden cards give a pile of coins, and 🔒 locked cards need their matching 🔑 key card cleared first. Use few draws for 3 stars. Coins buy upgrades, boosters (undo, +3 draws, wild), card backs with coin multis and pet eggs. Pets give coin multis, and sometimes flip you a free card that keeps your streak going. Rebirth resets your coins and upgrades but gives a coin multi for ever, and new worlds need rebirths. The leaderboard is total stars. Want the old game? Press Classic table.
