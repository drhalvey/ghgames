Drag on the table to aim (or tap a ball to aim straight at it). Pull the power bar on the right down and let go to shoot, or drag backwards from the white ball like pulling a cue. The guide line shows what your white ball will hit first. Tap the spin ball for top spin, back spin or side. Arrow keys aim and Space shoots on a keyboard. Fouls (potting the white, hitting the wrong ball first, or nothing touching a cushion) give your opponent ball in hand: drag the white ball to place it. Win games for coins to buy new cues and cloths.
