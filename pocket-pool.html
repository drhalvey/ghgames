Pick a table, then solve it in as few shots as you can. Tap or drag on the table to aim (the dotted line shows where the white ball goes), then pull the power bar down and let go to shoot. You can also drag back from the white ball like a slingshot. Keyboard: arrows to aim, hold Space to charge, let go to shoot. Goals: pot all the red balls, pot the gold ball after it goes through a portal, pot 1, 2 and 3 in order, or sink the white ball in the gold hole. Bumpers bounce, ramps launch balls over low hedges, portals teleport balls, and a coloured gate only lets balls of its own colour through. Potting the white ball in a normal hole costs a shot. Use par shots or fewer for 3 stars. Coins buy cues (more power and longer aim lines), chalk (more coins) and cue balls with coin multis. Pets give coin multis plus extra shots or longer aim lines. Rebirth resets coins, cues and chalk for a coin multi that lasts for ever, and new worlds need rebirths. The leaderboard is total stars.
