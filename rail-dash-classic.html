How to play

  • Change track: swipe left or right (or ⬅️ ➡️ / A D). Jump: swipe up (⬆️ / W / Space). Roll: swipe down (⬇️ / S). Swipe down in the air to slam back to the ground.
  • Trains: hit the front and it is game over. Clip the side and you stumble. Stumble twice quickly and Bolt the guard bot catches you.
  • Trains with a ramp can be run up. Run along the roofs for coins and jump from roof to roof.
  • Trains with flashing lights are moving towards you. Get out of their track!
  • Red barriers: jump. Yellow signs: roll under.
  • Power-ups: 🧲 Magnet pulls in coins, 🛡️ Shield saves you from one crash, 🚀 Rocket flies you over everything, ✖2 doubles your coins.
  • Missions: finish them for bonus coins. You always have three.
  • You get faster the longer you last. Your score is distance times your score multi.
