How to play: tap a shop card to buy a unit. Buy two of the same and they merge into a stronger one (up to 5 stars). Tap a unit on the board or bench, then tap a square or a bench slot to move it. Tanks and fighters go at the front, archers, mages and healers at the back. Drag the 3D view to turn the camera, pinch or scroll to zoom. Units from the same faction give bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 (Rift-born at 1, 2 and 3). Win rounds to knock the other side's hearts down to 0. Campaign: 25 worlds of 10 levels with a boss on every 10th. Public Arena: fight teams that real players have used, win trophies and climb the leaderboard. Rebirth after beating the boss shown on the Rebirth screen: more coins, more damage, an extra team slot and a new Rift-born unit each time.
