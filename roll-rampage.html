Gobbled!

Mouse: point where to roll, hold the mouse button to boost. Keyboard: WASD or arrows to roll, hold Space to boost. iPad: drag on the left side to roll, hold BOOST. Boosting burns a little mass. You can swallow a ball that is a bit smaller than you; bigger balls swallow you. Power-ups: ⚡ Speed, 🛡️ Shield (blocks one swallow), 🍄 Giant, 🧲 Magnet, ❄️ Freeze (stops nearby balls), ✨ Double Growth. Boss balls swallow anything that touches them, unless you are boosting: boost into a boss to crack it, and smash it for a mountain of golden food. Coins buy upgrades, worlds (each with much bigger food), skins and pet eggs. Skins and pets multiply your mass. Rebirth costs coins and resets coins and upgrades, but adds +1x mass and +0.5x coins for ever. In the Public Arena everyone starts the same size, and your multis make you grow faster. The leaderboard is most balls swallowed in one life.
