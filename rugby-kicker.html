How to play

  • The arrow swings up and down. Tap (or press space) to lock the angle.
  • Then the power bar goes up and down. Tap to stop it. The gold bit at the top is a PERFECT kick.
  • The higher the ball goes, the more Wins you get.
  • Get it over the posts for double Wins. Kick too straight up and it won't reach them!
  • Watch the wind. It blows the ball towards the posts or away from them.
  • Spend Wins on upgrades and new stadiums to kick into the clouds, into space and past the Moon.
  • Hard mode is trickier but pays way more Wins.
