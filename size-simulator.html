Eat things smaller than you to grow. Eat smaller players too, but watch out for bigger ones! Friends in the same world play together.

Pick a colour and a world, then press PLAY.

Upgrades

Every bite earns coins. Spend them here.

Worlds

Each world costs coins to unlock and makes you grow faster. Unlocked worlds stay unlocked, even after a rebirth.

Pets

Hatch eggs to get pets. Tap a pet to equip it (3 at first, more with Pet Slots). Pets multiply how fast you grow. Better worlds have better eggs. Fuse 3 pets of the same rarity to make one of the next rarity, all the way up to rarity 120: Absolute!

How to play

  • Move with the mouse, arrow keys or WASD. On iPad, hold and drag on the game.
  • Eat anything smaller than you. The bigger you get, the bigger the things you can eat.
  • PvP: if you are at least 1.5 times someone's size, run into them to eat them. If someone bigger eats you, you shrink and respawn.
  • King Chonk 👑 is always the biggest in the world, so run! He is a bit slower than you. Little Pip 🐣 is always the smallest, so anyone can eat him.
  • Boost (Space or the yellow button) gives a burst of speed to chase or escape.
  • Rebirthing needs 1Dc coins. 1Dc gives 1 rebirth, 1Ud gives 200, and every 1,000 times more coins gives 200 times more rebirths. It resets your size, coins and upgrades but makes you grow faster forever. Worlds and pets are kept.
  • Events start every 20 minutes to 2 hours and last 10 minutes. They multiply your growing and coins. Rarer events are bigger: up to x100!
  • Claim your daily reward every day. Don't miss a day or your streak starts again!
