SKY BOUNCE

Every bounce sends you higher, and your Bounce Power turns each jump into more and more metres. Grab coins on the way up, buy upgrades and climb through the clouds to space and beyond!

Whoops!

You bounce by yourself! Steer with the arrow keys or A and D, move the mouse, or hold the left or right side of the screen. Go off one side to pop out of the other. Every bounce climbs a few blocks, and your Bounce Power says how many metres each block is worth, so more power means much bigger heights. Plain platforms are safe, arrow ones slide, cracked ones snap, pale ones vanish after one bounce, springs, trampolines and ⚡ boost pads send you flying. Grab rockets, whirly caps, magnets, shields and coin showers. Tap a flying puff (or press Space) to throw a star at it, or land on its head. Reaching a new zone unlocks it as a checkpoint you can start from in Worlds. Coins are worth more in higher zones and buy upgrades, pet eggs and bouncer skins. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades but multiplies your Bounce Power for ever, and pets and skins multiply it too. The leaderboard is your highest height ever.
