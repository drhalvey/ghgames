SKY BOUNCE

You bounce by yourself! Steer left and right with the arrow keys, A and D, the mouse, or by holding the left or right side of the screen. Go off one side to pop out of the other. Tap a flying puff or press Space to throw a star at it, or land right on its head!

Whoops!

Your bouncer jumps on its own. Steer with the arrow keys or A and D, move the mouse, or hold the left or right half of the screen. Plain platforms are safe, ones with arrows slide, cracked ones snap in half, and pale see-through ones vanish after one bounce. Springs and pink trampolines send you flying. Grab a rocket pack or a whirly cap for a free ride up. Tap a flying puff (or press Space, W or Up) to throw a star, or stomp on its head. Collect coins to buy new bouncers and worlds. New best heights earn PhatCoin.
