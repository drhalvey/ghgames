Eat food to grow taller. Get tall enough to ride up the giant towers, then JUMP! Steer through rings, do flips, belly flop into the pool, bounce on trampolines and hit the bullseye for huge coins and trophies. Race bots or Dad to the ground, unlock 100 worlds, hatch pets and rebirth!

RACE TO
THE GROUND

Everyone jumps off the Race Tower at once. First to touch the ground wins! Hold DIVE to fall faster, fly through rings for a speed boost and dodge birds, balloons and storm clouds (they slow you down).

Son vs Dad is 2 players on one device with a split screen. Son: W A S D to steer, Space or G to dive, F for tricks. Dad: arrow keys to steer, L or Enter to dive, K for tricks. On iPad each player drags on their own half and uses their own DIVE button. Upgrades are switched off so it is fair.

YOU WIN!

Tap EAT (or press C, or click the game) to grow taller. Walk with W A S D or the arrow keys, Space to jump, drag to look around. Step on a glowing lift in front of a tower: if you are tall enough it carries you to the top. Then JUMP OFF! While falling, steer with W A S D or the joystick, hold DIVE (Space) to curl into a cannonball and fall faster, and press TRICK (F) for flips and silly moves that add to your multi. Land on the bullseye (the middle is x10), cannonball into the pool (x5) or belly flop it (x3), or bounce on the trampoline (every bounce adds x1). Coins buy bigger bites, auto eat, air control, dive speed, lucky rings, pets and new worlds. Trophies buy skins (coin multi) and trails (trophy multi). Rebirth for x3 height and x3 coins every time. 🏁 Race bots, or play Son vs Dad on one device. Your game saves by itself.
