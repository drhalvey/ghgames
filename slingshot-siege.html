SLINGSHOT SIEGE

The grumpy Glumps hide in forts of wood, stone, metal, gold and diamond. Fling your critters to smash them, earn coins from every block, upgrade your slingshot and hatch rare critters to build the ultimate siege team!

Some Glumps are still grumbling. Try a different angle!

Drag anywhere on the game (mouse or finger) and pull back away from the fort, then let go. Pull further for more power. Tap the screen or press Space while a critter flies to use its trick: Pip just bonks, Trio splits, Zoom dashes, Thud drops like a rock, Kaboom goes pop, Mega Boomer goes MEGA pop and Golden Gus slams down for double coins. Every block you smash pays coins. Coins upgrade your slingshot (power, extra critters, stronger tricks, coin bonus and a longer aiming line), hatch critter eggs (rarer eggs give rarer critters, and duplicates level a critter up) and unlock new worlds: Meadow, Desert, Snowy Peaks, Castle Hill, Volcano and Space, with metal, gold and diamond blocks. Your critter team is the critters you equip. Each world ends with a boss castle and its King Glump. When you beat a fort, the Glumps rebuild it tougher (but worth more) for your next visit. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades but keeps worlds, levels, skins and critters, and gives a bigger multi for ever. The leaderboard is your total stars.
