Gobble glowing pellets to grow, and every so often you EVOLVE: worm, snake, eel, serpent, wyrm, dragon, sky dragon, star dragon and beyond. Each new form unlocks powers like dash, coil traps, tail whip and fire breath to steal pellets from the computer snakes.

Popped!

Mouse: point where to slither, hold the button to boost. Keyboard: arrow keys or A and D to turn, Space to boost, Q to dash, E to tail whip, F to breathe fire. iPad: drag where you want to go and tap the power buttons. If your head bumps another snake's body you pop, so cut them off! From Evo 6, wrap your body right round a snake to pop it with a coil trap. Every pellet gives coins and evolution XP. Your evolution is kept when you pop; your length is not. Coins buy upgrades (speed, magnet, start length, growth, cooldowns, coins), new worlds where pellets are worth far more, skins with multis and pet eggs. Pets fly beside you and suck up pellets for you. Rebirth resets your coins and upgrades but gives a bigger multi for ever, and some worlds need rebirths. The leaderboard is the highest evolution reached.
