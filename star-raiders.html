Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys to fly, Space to dash. Mouse or iPad: drag anywhere on the game to steer with the joystick, and tap DASH to zoom out of trouble. Your ship shoots the closest alien all by itself. Grab the gold coins the aliens drop. After each wave pick 1 of 3 power-ups for this run: Spread Shot, Rapid Fire, Homing Missiles, Orbiting Shields, Drones, Laser, Nova Pulse, Piercing Shots and more. Every 5th wave a boss arrives! Coins are yours as soon as you grab them. They buy ship upgrades that last (open Upgrades between waves or from the pause menu), new galaxies (aliens are tougher but drop far more coins, and later galaxies need rebirths), new ships with multis and drone buddy eggs (drone buddies fly with you and shoot too). Rebirth resets coins and ship upgrades but keeps galaxies, ships and drone buddies, and adds +1x damage and coins for ever. The leaderboard is the highest wave you reach.
