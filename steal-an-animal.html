Tap an animal on the road to buy it. Run into other bases, grab an animal and bring it home!

How to play

  • Tap or hold on the map to run there (or use the arrow keys / WASD).
  • Animals walk along the road. Tap one to buy it. It sits in your base and makes coins every second.
  • Run into another base, touch an animal to grab it, then carry it back into your base to keep it. Watch out for the owner and their guard animals!
  • Robbers will try to steal from you too. Touch them to get your animal back, buy guard animals, or lock your base.
  • Shiny is ×2, Golden ×5, Diamond ×10 and Rainbow ×25.
  • There are 8 galaxies with 25 worlds each (200 worlds). Buy new worlds for better animals, but every world costs way, way more than the last. Every world you own doubles everything you earn. Each rebirth doubles your money forever.
  • Tap one of your own animals to sell it.
  • Use the 🍀 Luck Upgrader to get rarer animals and more mutations. Luck keeps when you rebirth.
  • There are 48 kinds of animal, 8 in every rarity.
  • 🪙 PhatCoin is the coin for all of GH Games. Get 2 for stealing from a computer base, 5 for stealing from a friend, 25 for a rebirth, a daily bonus, and up to 100 for being in today's top 10. Spend it in the PhatCoin Shop.
