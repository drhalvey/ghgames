How to play

You are a person pretending to be a T-rex, so your arms are tiny. Get right up close to land a Tiny Slap, lunge in with a big Chomp, spin round with a Tail Whip when they are next to you, and when your roar bar is full, ROAR in their face to stun them. Knock their health to zero and they flop over and go extinct. Best of 3 rounds wins.

One player: W A S D or the arrow keys to move. F or J slap, G or K chomp, H or L tail whip, R or I roar.
Two players, one keyboard: left player W A S D with F G H R, right player the arrows with J K L I.
iPad: drag your side of the screen to move and tap your four buttons. With two players, each of you gets one side of the screen.

Win against the computer for 🦴 bones. Bones buy new dinos (each fights differently), costumes (each gives a bones multi) and upgrades. Every 10th level is a boss.
