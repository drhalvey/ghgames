Move with W A S D or the arrow keys, jump with Space, drag the game to look around (the mouse wheel zooms). FLICK is Q, E or F (or swing the mouse fast): it whips the camera round, which is what makes the glitches work. LAUGH is L. 🔒 SHIFT LOCK (the Shift key or the button) works like Roblox: the camera sits over your shoulder, you always face where the camera looks and the mouse is locked so you just move it to look (Esc gives you the mouse back). On iPad: drag the left side to move, drag the right side to look, and use the JUMP, FLICK and LAUGH buttons. 📊 shows the difficulty chart and which glitch each tier needs, ❓ shows how to do the glitch on this floor, 🏢 takes you back to any floor you have reached. Your floor saves by itself. The glitches are this game's own versions of the famous Roblox ones, so they work a bit differently to Roblox.
