Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D. iPad: swipe anywhere on the game in the direction you want to go. Step off your land to draw a trail, then get back home to claim everything inside the loop. If a rival touches your trail while you are out, or you cross your own trail, your round ends (a Trail Shield saves you once, and pets guard your trail too). Run over a rival's trail to knock them out and grab their kingdom. Your land grows farms, then houses, then towers, which earn coins every second, even while you are out drawing. Coins buy upgrades (speed, trail shield, faster building, bigger starting land, castle levels and coins), banners with multis and pet eggs. Own 60% of the land to win a crown and a big payout. Rebirth resets coins and upgrades for a bigger multi for ever, and later worlds need rebirths. The leaderboard is the most crowns won.
