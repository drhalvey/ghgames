TIMING
Stop the needle in the green for a big jump. Each wall you clear pays wins, and taller walls pay more.

Worlds

Unlock new worlds with wins. Each world has its own eggs, and every world makes your jump and your wins 10 times bigger than the one before. You keep your pets, and you can travel back any time.

Rebirth

At 100B wins you can rebirth. Your wins go back to 0 and all your pets are deleted, except 1 pet you pick to keep. Every rebirth adds +1 to your rebirth multi, which boosts both your jump and the wins you earn. With more wins you get more rebirths at once: every 1,000 times more wins gives about 6.7 times more rebirths (1,000 ÷ 150).

    Egg Shop

    Spend wins on eggs. Every egg has 5 pets. The rarer the pet (lower %), the bigger its jump multi. Pricier eggs hold stronger pets.

    Your Pets

    Equip up to 4 pets. Their multis add together: jump multi = 1 + your 4 pets. Tap a pet to equip or unequip it.

    Craft

    Put 3 of the same pet in, get 1 pet out with double the jump multi. Crafted pets can be crafted again: Normal, then Big (x2), then Huge (x4), then Mega (x8).

    Leaderboard

    Your best wall goes on the board automatically. Same name as the other GH Games.

    Gifts

    Type a friend's in-game name and send them wins or one of your pets. You can only give what you have, and it leaves your game straight away. Gifts arrive while their game is open. Both of you need a name set.
