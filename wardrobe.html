XP, gear and crates are saved in your account so your look follows you into every game and onto every device.

How to get XP

  • Play any game: 1 XP a minute while you're playing.
  • Win: 1 XP (one every 15 seconds). Rebirth: 3 XP.
  • Up to 200 XP a day from playing, so it takes a while. That's what makes the Mythic gear special.
  • Daily quests: three a day for 30 to 50 XP each. Finish all three for a free crate key.
  • Every new level gives a crate key 🔑. Some levels give gear you can't buy anywhere.
  • Got a crate item you already have? You get half its value back in XP.
